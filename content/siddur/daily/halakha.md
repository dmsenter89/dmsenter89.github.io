This summary condenses the essential laws of Qiryath Shĕma' and Tĕfilla for one praying alone or with the members of his household. It is based on the Rambam's *Mishneh Torah* (Hilkhoth Qiryath Shĕma'; Hilkhoth Tĕfilla, chapters 1–7 and 10), in his own wording as far as possible and in condensed form, with additions from the Shulḥan 'Arukh, the Ben ʾIsh Ḥay (first year), and the Qiṣṣur Shulḥan 'Arukh, each cited by name.

The source is noted at the end of each topic by chapter and hălakha, following the numbering of the text before us, which sometimes differs from that of the printed editions. "See also" marks a nearby source that is not identical in every detail.
