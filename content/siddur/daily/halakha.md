This is a short summary of the essential laws of Qiryath Shĕma' and prayer (tĕfilla), for someone praying alone or with their household. It follows the Rambam's *Mishneh Torah* (Hilkhoth Qiryath Shĕma'; Hilkhoth Tĕfilla, chapters 1–7 and 10), keeping to his own wording as far as possible, in condensed form, with additions from the Shulḥan 'Arukh, the Ben ʾIsh Ḥay (Year 1) and the Qiṣṣur Shulḥan 'Arukh, each cited by name.

Each topic ends with its source, by chapter and hălakha, following the numbering of the edition used here, which sometimes differs from that of the printed editions. "See also" marks a nearby source that doesn't match in every detail.
