Rambam describes the afternoon service in *Mishneh Torah, Hilkhoth Tefilla* 7:18: begin seated with Tehilla LeDavid (Psalm 145), then rise and pray the Minha Amida standing. When finished, sit, bow low in supplication, sit up and add a little more entreaty according to one's ability, then return to one's affairs.
