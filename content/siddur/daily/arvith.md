The order of 'Arvith: say the Shĕma' with the bĕrakhoth before and after it, go straight from the bĕrakha of redemption into the 'Ămidha, and pray standing. When you finish, sit for a moment before you leave. Adding personal pleas after 'Arvith is praiseworthy.

The time for the evening Shĕma' runs from ṣeʾth hakkokhavim (when the stars come out) until dawn.

Before praying, wash your hands with water up to the wrist, saying the bĕrakha *'Al Nĕṭilath Yadhayim* ("concerning the washing of the hands"), and only then pray.
