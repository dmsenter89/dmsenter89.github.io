Rambam describes the evening service in *Mishneh Torah, Hilkhoth Tefilla* 7:18: recite the Shema with its blessings before and after, join redemption directly to the Amida, and pray standing. When finished, sit briefly before leaving. One who adds supplication after Arvith is praiseworthy. And though the blessing Hashkivenu follows "who redeemed Israel," it does not interrupt between redemption and prayer — the two are counted as a single long blessing.
