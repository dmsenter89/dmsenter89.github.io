The order of Arvith: recite the Shema with its blessings before and after, join redemption directly to the Amida, and pray standing. When finished, sit briefly before leaving. One who adds supplication after Arvith is praiseworthy.

The time for the evening Shema is from יציאת הכוכבים until עמוד השחר. 

Before praying, one washes the hands with water up to the wrist, first reciting the blessing *'Al Nĕṭilath Yadhayim* ("concerning the washing of the hands"), and only then prays.
