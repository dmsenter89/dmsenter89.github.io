The Torah read over about three years in the ʾEreṣ Yisraʾel triennial cycle, one seder each Shabbath, with sĕdharim, hafṭaroth and ʿăliyyoth as calendared by Machon Shilo. Tap a seder to see its seven ʿăliyyoth. Each reading shows its opening words, and a whole seder its closing words too, so you can find the place in any Ḥumash. References and words follow *Miqra according to the Masorah*. For a daily study plan built on these sĕdharim, see the [Five-Day Seder Cycle](/siddur/appendix/study-cycles/five-day-seder-cycle/).
