A map of the Torah to learn by heart, in four zoom levels: the five books, each with one word; 21 sections, each a run of whole parashiyyoth; the 54 parashiyyoth, each with a poster word of one to three words; and, when you tap a parasha, its paragraphs. Learn the first two levels by heart, fill in the third over a year, and look up the fourth.

Turn on **Quiz me** to hide the words, then tap each blank to check yourself. Paragraph lines link to the verses on Sefaria. The § numbers are the sections of Rabbi Aryeh Kaplan's *The Living Torah*; the headings are reworded.
