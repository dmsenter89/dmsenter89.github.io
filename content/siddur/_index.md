🚧 This is a personal siddur page and still under construction. The main goal is to facilitate finding the core parts of tefillah when davening alone. Most apps have the entire communal service, leading to lots of scrolling which I find distracting. I try to add educational elements like transliterations and (external) links to audio to help with learning the melodies.
